Having a skill is one thing. Finding the right people to build with is another.

Across our campuses, there are students with valuable abilities.

Some can design. Some can develop software. Others understand marketing, finance, communication, engineering, fashion, agriculture and different areas of business.

But too often, these talents exist independently.

A student may have an excellent business idea but lack the people needed to execute it. Another may have the technical ability but struggle to find clients. Someone else may have the marketing knowledge but lack a reliable team to work with.

The challenge is not always the absence of talent. Sometimes, it is the absence of the right connections.

TeamBud was created to help bridge that gap. We believe students should have more opportunities to work together, build businesses and gain real-world experience before graduation.

The person behind the idea.

Onifade Sulaiman Ipesola, popularly known as Mr. Clarity, is a Computer Science student, young leader and digital entrepreneur with a strong interest in technology, entrepreneurship, digital innovation and helping people discover opportunities.

He has served as a Class Representative from 100 Level through 400 Level and as the NACOS General Secretary. Those roles put him in daily contact with students from different backgrounds, and taught him how much communication, teamwork and representation matter. Over time, they strengthened his belief that talent becomes more valuable when people learn to collaborate.

Beyond academics and student leadership, he works in digital marketing, brand development and digital skills education. He is the founder of Clarity Digital Academy, where his interest in helping people develop practical digital skills continues to shape his work.

The idea behind TeamBud came from an observation. There are talented students everywhere, but many struggle to find the right people to collaborate with, establish businesses and turn their individual abilities into sustainable opportunities. Some have the skills but lack business partners. Some have business ideas but lack the people to execute them. Others can deliver professional services but find it hard to build a complete team.

So he began to ask how students could be connected beyond their departments, institutions and areas of expertise.

What if a designer could easily find a developer?

What if a marketing student could connect with a business strategist?

What if students from different disciplines could come together, establish a brand, offer services and earn as a team?

That thinking became the foundation of TeamBud. The vision is to create an ecosystem where students do not have to wait until graduation to experience collaboration, business ownership and practical entrepreneurship. A place to connect, collaborate, create and build businesses together.

"I believe that some of the most valuable businesses of tomorrow can begin with students who decide to combine their abilities today. TeamBud is about making those connections possible."

Onifade Sulaiman Ipesola (Mr. Clarity), Founder
